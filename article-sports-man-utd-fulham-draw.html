Manchester United Rescue Late Point at Fulham

Verified Gerald Anthony · 4 min read
Manchester United fought back to draw 1-1 at Fulham
Share f 𝕏 wa tg

LONDON — Manchester United escaped with a 1-1 draw against Fulham at Craven Cottage on Sunday, September 20, after Matheus Cunha scored a late equaliser to prevent Michael Carrick's side from suffering a third consecutive defeat and extend the pressure surrounding United's early-season form.

Fulham had appeared on course to claim their first league victory of the season after United struggled to create clear chances for long periods of the match. The home side defended its advantage effectively before the visitors found a late route back into the contest.

Cunha's equaliser arrived in the 89th minute, giving United a point that would otherwise have appeared unlikely during much of the second half.

The result came at the end of a difficult run for United. The club had already lost the Manchester derby to Manchester City the previous weekend and had suffered a damaging League Cup exit against Brighton after taking a two-goal lead in that match. Another defeat at Fulham would have intensified scrutiny on Carrick before the extended international break.

Instead, the late goal changed the immediate outcome, although the performance left United with plenty to address during the break.

Fulham had entered the match still searching for their first league victory of the campaign and were close to securing it before Cunha's intervention. The result means Marco Silva's side will have to wait longer for its first three points despite spending most of the match in a position to claim them.

United's equaliser also highlighted the narrow margins that have characterised the club's start to the season. The team had opportunities to influence the game earlier but struggled to turn possession and attacking situations into clear chances.

For Carrick, the extended international break provides time to assess the team's performances and work on areas that have contributed to the inconsistent opening to the campaign. The pressure surrounding United is not solely about the result at Fulham — expectations were high before the season, and the sequence of results against City, Brighton and Fulham has increased attention on the team's ability to maintain consistency.

Cunha's late intervention nevertheless prevented a damaging defeat and gave United a point before the squad disperses for international duty. Fulham, meanwhile, can take encouragement from the performance despite failing to protect their lead, having controlled important periods of the match.

The draw leaves both teams with questions to answer when domestic football resumes. United will be expected to use the international break to address its recent defensive and attacking inconsistencies, while Fulham will continue searching for the victory that has so far remained out of reach.
