NFF Crisis Deepens as Ibrahim Gusau and Executive Committee Resign

Verified Stream26 Nigeria Desk · 4 min read
NIGERIA
A federation in transition, weeks before its own elective congress

Nigeria's football administration has entered a major transition after Nigeria Football Federation president Ibrahim Musa Gusau, general secretary Mohammed Sanusi, and members of the federation's executive committee resigned — only weeks before the previously scheduled NFF elective congress.

The resignations follow a difficult stretch for Nigerian football: the Super Eagles failed to qualify for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, and the Super Falcons subsequently missed qualification for the 2027 Women's World Cup. For the women's team, the miss was especially significant, since Nigeria had appeared at every Women's World Cup since the tournament began.

Gusau said the decision followed consultations with family, friends, and football stakeholders, framing it as an opening for structural change rather than a reaction to one bad result, describing it as making way for “a comprehensive and orderly reform process.”

The NFF has now moved into an interim arrangement: Emmanuel Ikpeme has been appointed to oversee the NFF Secretariat, with Ademola Olajire serving as interim deputy general secretary. The federation has suspended preparations for the September 27 elective congress, with a fresh electoral process expected once reforms are worked out with FIFA, CAF, government, and other stakeholders.

That leaves Nigerian football at a genuine fork in the road. The leadership vacuum could produce another cycle of political reshuffling — or it could become the start of real institutional reform. Stream26 News will continue following the transition as the reform process takes shape.

Still developing: The shape of the coming reform process, and who will lead the federation once it concludes, have not yet been determined.
